Building Repairs WebMCP / Fix This

Controlled demo SMS terms

Last updated August 30, 2026.

This is a private, time-bounded demonstration for one allowlisted participant. It is not a public messaging program, emergency service, or live tenant offering.

Opt-in

The allowlisted demo operator voluntarily enrolls by texting START to the private Fix This demo number supplied directly to them. Consent is not a condition of purchase or service. Do not share or publish the private demo number.

Messages

Building Repairs WebMCP may send transactional Fix This messages about the controlled repair case, including intake acknowledgements, requests for repair evidence or access details, safety and status updates, and an approved booking confirmation. Message frequency varies during the demo. Message and data rates may apply.

Stop, restart, and help

Reply STOP to stop automated messages. Reply START to opt back in after stopping. Reply HELP for help. Opt-out remains effective through controlled reset.

Availability

The controlled demo is available only during an announced test window and may be ended or reset at any time. Fix This is not emergency dispatch; call 911 for immediate danger.

Privacy

See the controlled demo privacy notice for what the demo processes, how it is used, and the 24-hour content-retention ceiling.
