Controlled demo privacy notice

Last updated September 1, 2026.

This notice covers the private, allowlisted Fix This controlled-live demo. The public demo on this site uses synthetic data and does not send real messages or calls.

Information used

The controlled demo may process the allowlisted participant's SMS and MMS content, repair photos, message and call delivery records, repair-case actions, consent and opt-out choices, and a post-consent voice transcript. Raw call audio is not stored. The campaign service number is public on the SMS consent evidence page. The participant's mobile number remains a private service binding and is not shown in the case or public evidence.

Why it is used

This information is used only to run the time-bounded repair demonstration: receive a report, gather relevant evidence, prepare manager review, contact the approved contractor after approval, and confirm the visit. It is not used for advertising or marketing.

Service providers and mobile information

Twilio, OpenAI, and the protected hosting provider process information only as needed to operate the demo. Mobile information and SMS opt-in data are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention

Controlled-demo case content is kept for no more than 24 hours and is deleted on a controlled reset. Opt-out, consent-withdrawal, and content-free replay records may remain until the controlled-live service is destroyed so that unwanted or duplicate contact is prevented. Provider systems may retain limited records under their own policies.

Messaging choices

Message frequency varies during the demo. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, START to opt back in, or HELP for help. Messaging consent is voluntary and is not a condition of purchase or service.
