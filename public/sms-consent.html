SMS consent evidence

Last updated September 1, 2026.

This page lets campaign reviewers verify the complete SMS invitation and keyword opt-in workflow. It does not enroll visitors; only the allowlisted tester may participate in the controlled demo.

Who sends messages, who receives them, and why

Building Repairs WebMCP sends low-volume transactional Fix This SMS and MMS messages. The only recipient is one allowlisted property-maintenance demo participant who voluntarily enrolls by keyword. Messages support that participant's active controlled repair case: report acknowledgement, requests for repair photos or access details, safety and status updates, manager-approved contractor scheduling, and booking confirmation. The program sends no marketing, lead generation, or public broadcasts.

Invitation shown before opt-in

Building Repairs WebMCP invites you to receive recurring Fix This transactional SMS and MMS about your controlled repair case. Text START to +14845926889. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of purchase or service. Review the SMS Terms and Privacy Notice before opting in.

Complete opt-in workflow

  1. The single allowlisted participant reviews the invitation above, including the published Fix This campaign service number.
  2. The participant voluntarily texts START from the allowlisted mobile number to +14845926889. SMS keyword is the only initial opt-in method; there is no website form, verbal, paper, QR-code, purchased-list, or imported-contact opt-in.
  3. Twilio replies: “Building Repairs WebMCP: Subscribed to Fix This repair-demo texts. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. HELP for help, STOP to opt out.”
  4. After opting out, the same participant may text START, YES, or UNSTOP to opt back in. STOP, CANCEL, END, OPTOUT, QUIT, REVOKE, STOPALL, or UNSUBSCRIBE stops automated messaging; HELP or INFO returns help without enrolling anyone.
